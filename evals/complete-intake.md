# Eval: Complete intake

**Scenario:** The user supplies a full, honest set of business and service facts for a
lawn maintenance page (Cedar & Stone Landscaping — 8 years, licensed/insured,
satisfaction guarantee, scope, process, preparation, timing, warm tone, and a
request-a-quote CTA).

**What good looks like:**
- A complete Markdown page, not an outline.
- Every claim traces to a supplied fact — no invented reviews, prices, or superlatives.
- "Serves Fairview and nearby towns" phrasing; no implied offices elsewhere.
- Warm, plain tone as requested.
- CTA points to the request-a-quote form.
- A Search presentation block (3 titles, 3 metas, slug, breadcrumb) is appended.

**Failure signals:** invented star ratings; "best/#1"; mechanical "lawn maintenance in
[City]" repetition; a city list; JSON-LD that wasn't requested.
