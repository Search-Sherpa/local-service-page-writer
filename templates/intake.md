# Business Profile format (output of onboarding)

When onboarding is complete, the skill produces a profile in roughly this shape. Save it
and reuse it for every future page — you'll only answer the small per-service questions
after this.

```markdown
# Business Profile: [Business name]

## Identity
- Business name: [name]
- Type / category: [e.g., residential plumbing]
- Service model: [visit location / travels to customer / both]

## Service area
- Based in / centered on: [city]
- Genuinely serves: [areas — "serves," not "located in"]
- Area constraints: [radius, seasons, travel limits, or "none stated"]

## Proof and trust (verified only)
- Licenses / certifications: [or "none provided"]
- Verifiable numbers: [years, reviews, rating, jobs — or "none provided"]
- Guarantees / warranties: [in the owner's words, or "none provided"]

## Voice and audience
- Ideal customer: [description]
- Tone: [friendly / professional / etc.]
- Avoid: [words, claims, competitors — or "none stated"]

## Call to action
- Primary action: [call / book / quote]
- Destination: [phone / link / form — exact detail if provided]

## Services to build pages for
- [service 1]
- [service 2]
- [service 3]
```
