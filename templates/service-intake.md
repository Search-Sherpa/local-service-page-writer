# Per-Service Questions (create-page mode)

Use this when a **Business Profile already exists** and you only need the facts specific to
*this one service*. Ask only what's missing, max 3 at a time. Skip anything already stated.

1. **The service** — exactly what is this page's service called? (e.g., "tankless water
   heater installation," not just "water heaters")
2. **What it includes / excludes** — what's covered in a typical job, and what isn't?
3. **The customer's problem** — what situation makes someone need this? What are the
   signs?
4. **The process** — what actually happens, step by step, from first contact to done?
5. **Preparation** — what should the customer do, clear, or provide beforehand?
6. **Timing** — anything honest you can say about how long it takes or when it's available?
   (Skip if you can't state it truthfully.)
7. **Options / variations** — different versions, tiers, or materials worth mentioning?
8. **Objections** — the top one or two worries customers have about this specific service?
9. **Proof for this service** — any real photos, before/after, reviews, or examples tied
   to *this* work? (Skip if none.)
10. **Follow-up** — any maintenance or related service that naturally comes after?

Everything the Business Profile already answers (area, licenses, tone, CTA, etc.) is
inherited automatically — don't re-ask it.
