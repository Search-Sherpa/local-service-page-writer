# Per-Service Intake: Water Heater Repair

> Entirely fictional example. These service-level facts, together with
> `sample-business-profile.md`, are the source inputs for `sample-service-page.md`.

- Exact service: Residential tank water heater repair
- Includes: Diagnosis, repair recommendation, approved component repair or replacement,
  operating check, and explanation of findings
- Excludes: Tankless systems and commercial systems
- Common customer situations: No hot water, inconsistent temperature, unusual tank
  noises, discolored hot water, pilot or burner trouble, or water around the unit
- Repair-versus-replacement approach: Recommend repair when the tank is sound and repair
  is economically sensible; explain replacement when the tank body has failed or repair
  would offer poor value
- Appointment process: Inspect and diagnose, explain recommendation, obtain approval,
  perform the repair, test operation, and review findings
- Customer preparation: Clear a path and work area, note symptoms and when they started,
  and have an adult available to approve work
- Safety note supplied by business: If the unit is actively leaking, customers may shut
  off its water supply only if they can do so safely
- Availability: Standard business-hour appointments; no same-day guarantee
- Fuel types serviced: Not yet confirmed
- Repair warranty: Not yet confirmed
- Follow-up service: Water heater installation when replacement is the better option
